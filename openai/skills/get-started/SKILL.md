# Getting Started with Gryz in ChatGPT

Gryz gives you one place to keep the context you and your AI assistants rely on: memories, notes, skills, and documents.

## First Steps

1. **Save a memory**: Ask "Remember that <a preference>." Gryz stores it with `remember`.
2. **Recall it later**: In a new chat, ask "What do you remember about me?" (`recall`).
3. **Start a running list**: "Start a note called <name>" (`create_note`), then "Add <item> to <name>" (`append_note`).
4. **Publish a document**: "Publish this as a private Gryz doc" (`publish_document`) — returns a link.

## What You Can Do

- **Remember and recall**: Save durable facts, such as preferences, project decisions, and working context, then recall them in a later conversation or from another assistant connected to the same Gryz account.
- **Keep notes**: Create running lists, decision logs, and drafts, then add to them over time without rewriting what is already there.
- **Use skills**: List and read the reusable instructions saved in your account, and create, copy, or update your own.
- **Publish and edit documents**: Turn Markdown or HTML into a Gryz document with a shareable link, find and read your existing documents, edit one section without rewriting the rest, and view or restore earlier versions.

## Example Prompts

- "Remember that I prefer concise bullet-point summaries, then show me what you remember about me."
- "Find my Q4 launch plan in Gryz and summarize the key dates."
- "Add \"confirm demo video URL\" to my launch checklist note in Gryz."

## Manage Everything

Visit https://www.gryz.ai to review, edit, or delete memories, notes, skills, and documents in your dashboard.
